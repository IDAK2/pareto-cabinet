# Design identity: instrument cabinet

Pareto Cabinet looks like a dark optical bench: constraint lamps on the left, a glowing frontier wire across the center, and physical specimen drawers below. A candidate does not become a generic card; it is filed as a labelled metal slide with three punched penalty apertures.

The palette is graphite, electric chartreuse, cold cyan, and brushed aluminium. Typography uses compressed industrial headings and monospaced instrument labels. Opening an assay and inserting a specimen happen in separate sliding trays so the screen never becomes a standard stacked form.
